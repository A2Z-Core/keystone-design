# 02 — Factions & Politics

This section defines the geopolitical landscape, sovereign nations, factions, treaties, alliances, and ideological conflicts across the world of Keystone.

---

## Key Topics to Establish:
1. **Major World Factions**: Are there two opposing superpowers (e.g. Alliance vs Horde style), a tripartite balance of power, or independent city-states?
2. **Neutral Organizations**: Mercenary companies, druidic circles, arcane colleges, and explorer guilds that operate across borders.
3. **Casus Belli & Ongoing Wars**: The flashpoints, contested resource zones, and philosophical divides driving active PvP and world tension.

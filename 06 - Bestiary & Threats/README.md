# 06 — Bestiary & Threats

This section catalogues the wildlife, monstrous predators, corrupted beings, dungeon bosses, world raid entities, and ancient cosmic threats in the Keystone world.

---

## Key Topics to Establish:
1. **Indigenous Fauna & Flora**: Beasts, predators, and creatures that populate various biomes.
2. **Sentient Antagonists**: Bandit syndicates, cults, splinter factions, and barbarian tribes.
3. **Planar / Corrupted Entities**: Abominations, undead, void horrors, and corrupted elementals.
4. **World Bosses & Dungeon Encounters**: Legendary entities requiring cooperative group or raid coordination.

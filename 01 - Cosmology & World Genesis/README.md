# 01 — Cosmology & World Genesis

This section details the fundamental nature of reality in Keystone, the creation of the cosmos, the primordial forces that govern existence, and the overarching concept of the **Keystones**.

---

## Key Topics to Establish:
1. **The Origin of the Cosmos**: How the universe came to be and the ancient architects/titans/celestials who shaped it.
2. **The Nature of the Keystones**: What are the legendary Keystones? (Are they cosmic anchors holding reality together, ancient artifacts of power, planar conduits, or fragments of a shattered world core?)
3. **The Magic & Energy Wheel**:
   * **Arcane / Aether**: Order, intellect, mathematics of reality (Resource: *Mana*).
   * **Primal / Blood**: Kinetic force, instinct, adrenaline, and battle momentum (Resource: *Rage / Energy*).
   * **Divine / Light**: Faith, justice, celestial order (Resource: *Holy Power / Devotion*).
   * **Shadow / Void**: Entropy, decay, sacrifice, and the unknown depths (Resource: *Soul / Corruption*).
   * **Elemental**: Fire, Earth, Water, Wind, and Spirit (Resource: *Maelstrom / Harmony*).

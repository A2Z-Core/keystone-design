# 05 — World Atlas & Geography

This section documents the physical geography of the world, continents, regional climate zones, major cities, and starting lands for new players.

---

## Key Topics to Establish:
1. **World Structure & Continents**: Number of landmasses, oceans, archipelagos, or celestial floating shards.
2. **Starting Zones (Level 1–10)**: Safe introduction zones with regional narrative arcs and local threats.
3. **Contested Mid/End-game Zones**: Wild frontiers, contested resource valleys, and high-level regions.
4. **Capitals & Major Hubs**: Trade ports, citadels, and sanctums where players trade, craft, and socialize.
